Chapter 01

Types of Exercise-Induced Muscle Soreness

Definition

Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS) is muscle pain that begins 12-24 hours after exercise, peaks at 24-48 hours, and can last up to 10-14 days.

Acute Muscle Soreness

Occurs during or immediately after exercise. Caused by ischemia (lack of oxygen) and metabolite buildup (lactic acid, potassium). Felt as burning/aching. Subsides quickly with active recovery.

Delayed (DOMS)

Appears 12-24 hours post-exercise. Peak at 24-48 hours. Caused by microtrauma to muscle fibers. Eccentric contractions cause the most severe symptoms.

Chapter 02

What Causes DOMS? (Theories Examined)

Disproven Theories
01

Metabolic Waste Theory

Lactic acid buildup was thought to cause DOMS. DISPROVED: Studies show lactic acid clears within 1 hour post-exercise.

→
02

Muscle Spasm Theory

Suggested pain-spasm cycle caused ongoing soreness. DISPROVED: EMG studies showed no increase in muscle activity.

→
03

Current Understanding

ACCEPTED: Mechanical microtrauma to muscle fibers and connective tissue causes inflammation, degeneration, and edema.

Chapter 06

Treatment: What Works? (Evidence Mixed)

✗ Limited Evidence

✗ NSAIDs - may mask pain, not speed recovery
✗ Cryotherapy - reduces CK but not soreness
✗ Massage - widespread use, but NOT proven
✗ Electrical stimulation

✓ Some Evidence

✓ Light concentric exercise - may reduce soreness
✓ Compression sleeves - faster recovery, less swelling
✓ Topical salicylate creams - analgesic effect
✓ CONTINUING TRAINING does NOT worsen damage

★ KEY INSIGHT: The best "treatment" is prevention through consistent training - muscle adapts!

Chapter 07

Myths vs Facts

❌ Myth

"No pain = No gain"
If you're not sore the next day, your workout wasn't effective.

✓ Fact

Muscle growth isn't measured by soreness. Progress comes from consistent training over time.

❌ Myth

"Stretch Before Exercise"
Static stretching before working out prevents soreness.

✓ Fact

Dynamic warmups are better. Static stretching may actually reduce strength temporarily.

❌ Myth

"More Pain = More Damage"
If it hurts badly, you're injuring your muscles.

✓ Fact

DOMS is normal. Sharp or sudden pain is different - stop if you feel this!

Chapter 08

Key Takeaways
